    Our Philosophy

    How we make them

    From the cream we buy to the way we freeze the bars, we keep it simple.

    01 / 03

    Thick, Not Watery

    Heavy cream, Mexican crema, and condensed milk. No water base. That's what gives the bite a fudgy feel instead of crunchy ice.

    02 / 03

    Real Fruit, Not Syrup

    We blend real fruit, then strain it through a fine sieve or cheesecloth so the texture stays silky. Strawberries lose their seeds, mango loses its fibers. No concentrates, no syrups.

    03 / 03

    Like Abuelita Made It

    Small batches in a 20-cavity stainless steel mold. Frozen overnight, never rushed. The way it's done at home, just at higher volume.
